## APEX Rising

APEX operates a multichain swap interface with public DEX statistics, a Cronos APEX token staking page, skill games and tournaments. Robinhood Chain appears in the swap interface.

The publisher marks perpetuals and predictions as practice, and tokenized stocks, card payments and APEX Evolution as coming soon.

[Source 1](https://apexrising.io/swap) · [Source 2](https://apexrising.io/dex/stats) · [Source 3](https://apexrising.io/play) · [Source 4](https://apexrising.io/staking) · [Source 5](https://apexrising.io/markets) · [Source 6](https://apexrising.io/)
