### Collection groups

- Hood Vandalz → Hood Syndicate, explicitly linked by the official Syndicate page.
- Pixel Hood Arcade → Pixel Hood Minis and PIXEL HOOD CLAN, confirmed through Pixelord’s creator profile and individual collection pages.
- CannaCats V2 → CannaCats, both listed by the official CannaCat creator profile.
- Animal Pharm → Pharm Creations, Cats On Rugs, and Pixel Cats on Drugs, directly connected through the artist’s sites.
- TMO: Initiates → older Mallard Order collections linked by the official project Linktree. Cardano and Bitcoin retain their actual chain labels.

A collection group is not a claim about shared smart contracts. Similar artwork or a similar name alone is not sufficient proof of association. No price, volume, market-rank, revenue, or partnership claims are published.

### Availability distinctions

PRICED OUT has no confirmed separate website or game. CannaCats collection destinations are included without guessing an external app. WFit’s companion roadmap is not presented as fully released. Animal Pharm’s site says new Pharm Creations submissions are closed. Gorilla Scouts is labeled a paper beta, and Rillaz’s free-chip dice game is not described as real-money wagering. TMO’s older collection destinations were creator-linked; some marketplace pages returned HTTP403 to the research client.
