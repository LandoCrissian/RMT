# LAMMB Money Lab

The Money Lab is linked from the LAMMB Vault. It is a browser-local scenario model, not a store, treasury wallet, mining orderbook, access editor, or payout contract. No data is written to D1, and no connected wallet is required. The live Vault gate remains five LAMMBs in one wallet. All public presets are fictional operations examples, not actual holder positions, bookings or earnings.

## Calculation boundaries

- **Royalties** are completed-sale volume multiplied by the modeled royalty rate and the estimated proportion of volume paying the full fee. The price input is an average executed price assumption, not a live floor feed. [OpenSea creator earnings](https://support.opensea.io/en/articles/8867026-how-do-i-set-creator-earnings-on-opensea)
- **Sponsored placements** are separate RMT revenue. Gross starts with proposed $5, $15 and $30 monthly rates. Subtract ad fees, costs and cash reserves before assigning any proceeds. Negative net ad revenue remains a visible shortfall and generates zero mining budget. Actual future ads should be distinguished clearly from organic listings. [FTC native advertising guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/native-advertising-guide-businesses)
- **Mining budget** is positive net ad proceeds plus an editable fraction of creator royalties. Its default royalty allocation is zero. This figure is available funding in the model, never mined Bitcoin or an approved transfer.
- **Pilot P&L** is hypothetical gross pool recovery minus the modeled fee, rental spend and other mining costs. Break-even is gross proceeds before pool fees. The historical-style starting example is not a quote. [Braiins rewards and fees](https://academy.braiins.com/braiins-pool/rewards-and-payouts)
- **Holder scenario** uses only positive pilot profit multiplied by a proposed holder share (default zero). The sample wallet receives a proportional illustration only if it meets the selected gate and is part of the entered eligible supply. Sub-cent figures are shown without creating payment amounts. No payouts are configured or claimable.
- **Membership** compares one, five, ten and twenty-five tokens. The maximum wallet count equals whole collection supply divided by the selected threshold, rounded down. It is not a live count, and no threshold control affects access.

The default scenario gives $23.50 in gross royalties from 100 sales at $2.35 with full fee coverage. Six modeled sponsors bring $85 gross and $75 after $10 of costs and reserves. The separate $20 pilot returns $19.53 after modeled pool fees, which is a $0.47 loss before any extra costs. Its modeled holder pool is zero.
